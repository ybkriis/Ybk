# Angle: Escaping GTA 6's wanted system

## Spine
In GTA 5 you escaped by getting out of sight. In GTA 6 you escape by becoming someone they're not looking for.
(GTA 5 asked *can they see you?* GTA 6 asks *do they know who you are?*)

## Systems → chapters (each causes the next)
1. **The Witness**: no witness, no crime. → a witness doesn't just report a crime, they describe a person.
2. **The Description**: heat icons: clothes, car, appearance, the couple. → people forget, but cameras don't.
3. **The Camera**: shop CCTV, the witness you can't outrun. → once they have you on record, the stars stop being a timer and start being a status.
4. **The Stars**: hollow, white, flashing, red; six of them. → stars gone doesn't mean you're free…
5. **The Heat**: lingering heat, the area remembers, snap-back. → so running isn't enough. You have to change.
6. **The Escape**: the new playbook: break sight, leave the zone, change clothes, swap the car, split up. → except one level where none of it works.
7. **The Sixth Star**: the payoff.

## Cold-open contrasts (draft)
- GTA 5: rob a store, stars before you reach the door. GTA 6: rob a store with nobody watching, and nothing happens.
- GTA 5: lose the cops, drive the same car past them. GTA 6: they're looking for that car.
- GTA 5: five stars. GTA 6: six.
- GTA 5: cops remember nothing. GTA 6: "Our dispatch is extremely specific in what it knows."

## Open loops
1. Structural: "Every one of these systems feeds the next. By the end you'll see why the oldest GTA trick, outrunning the cops, barely works anymore."
2. Withheld item: "There's one level in GTA 6 where, according to the guides, there's no escape at all. I'll show you that at the end." → Chapter 7.
